# Voi Portfolio Lab 7.2

A student decision simulator for Voi Oslo, with a frozen report case and transparent assumptions.

[Open the report case](https://kingen560.github.io/voi-portfolio-engine/#case=report-hub-first) · [Explore ordinary defaults](https://kingen560.github.io/voi-portfolio-engine/) · [Frozen report evidence](https://kingen560.github.io/voi-portfolio-engine/paper_evidence_pack/)

## Use it

Choose an investment and implementation plan. The headline, alternatives, funding checks and uncertainty analysis use that same plan. Pilot then scale and direct rollout are compared explicitly. Both Hub & Book and Voi Together, including their design variants, remain available.

**Report case.** The paper proposes Hub & Book first, through a pilot and conditional expansion. The complete named configuration uses 20% additional dedicated placement as an operating objective; 25% is the reference and 30% the downside. It does not force booking to win the economic comparison. Loading the case replaces current inputs explicitly; Undo restores them.

**Evidence.** The frozen pack contains full configurations, monthly economic and cash results, source/assumption register, methods, pilot protocol, three reusable figures and a PDF. Every output shares a configuration/source hash and manifest. Current-case JSON/CSV/evidence-note exports capture the selected investment and plan.

## Limits

The estimated 124,822 active Oslo customers are a constructed planning scope. The 72-person convenience sample identifies exploratory needs, not population segment shares, booking demand or willingness to pay. Retention and other unmeasured relationship benefits start at zero. Operating costs, supply overlap, demand response and internal capacity requirements remain assumptions. Resource opportunity cost is distinguished from additional cash expenditure.

No calibrated forecast, causal effect or observed probability of success is claimed. No raw respondent records, private course materials or model_work files are included. All calculations run in the browser without analytics or a data feed.
