How much rests on future relationships?

Current-input options on the selected implementation path. Switches off retention, repeat, upgrade and placement-spillover effects together. Guest activation stays an explicit, separate assumption. Differences interact and are not additive causal attribution.

What if competitors respond?

Copy: entered share of demand and relationship benefit remains. Price: a relative price disadvantage changes demand through the response coefficients. Fade: retention, repeat and spillover advantages fall linearly to zero. No separate defence premium is added.

Does a higher price still look better?

Hub & Book with current inputs on the selected implementation path, at three fees and four fee-response coefficients. The coefficients are unestimated; zero response is a stress, not a price recommendation.
