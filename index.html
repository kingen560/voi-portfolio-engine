Definitions, assumptions and all cases

Future-behaviour benefits: 0%, 50%, 100% of your inputs, including guest activation. Booking price response: 0, 0.5, 1 and your current value, under linear and logit forms sharing reference adoption and local elasticity. Segment weights: current, equal, or target-segment weights halved then all weights normalised. Baseline lapse rates: ×0.7, ×1, ×1.3; engagement recalibrates as specified. These are deliberate stress levels, not estimated uncertainty intervals.

03 / INSPECT · DELIVERY

What must change, and how the pilot decides

Every capability has an owner, a cost and a ready month, and an offer launches only once its capabilities exist. The pilot then measures the assumptions that matter and applies a stop rule in which the setup already spent no longer counts.

What makes the pilot evidence credible?

The simulation's relative measurement error is a scenario device. It is not a power calculation, a confidence interval or the expected information from this proposed study.

03 / INSPECT · EVIDENCE

Follow the numbers.

Open a worked calculation to see how an input reaches the result. Definitions, population scope and the full evidence register are available below. Use the tabs above for uncertainty or implementation.

What this model establishes — and what it does not

This is a conditional decision model. Occasional, Frequent, Subscriber and Inactive are usage states; they do not measure trust or cooperation in the relationship typology of Johnson & Selnes. Monthly transitions and treatment effects are scenario inputs. A 36-month incremental value has no terminal value and is not a complete estimate of Voi's lifetime customer equity.

Cross-language checks verify arithmetic and implementation. External validation against Voi outcomes, a fitted price-response model and causal treatment-effect estimation remain to be done. Monte Carlo ranges describe assumed input uncertainty; the assumption challenge separately tests demand structure, future-benefit channels, segment weights and baseline dynamics.

Methodological inspirations: Johnson & Selnes (2004); RAND Robust Decision Making; Briggs et al. (2012); Eddy et al. (2012). The decision-analysis guidance is adapted from other sectors; it supplies no Voi effect sizes. Train (2009) motivates the unestimated logit probability form. BG/NBD estimation and spatial fleet optimisation are deferred until transaction and spatial data exist.

The ordinary case assumes the current market structure. Revisit inputs and mechanisms if competition, customer needs, regulation or technology change; an unexpected disruption may require a new model.

What the numbers rest on

The baseline is calibrated to Voi's 2025 revenue, Ruter's trip count and our survey's usage mix. Each input says where its number comes from and, separately, what supports the mechanism behind it.

Population scope and generalisation

The 72-person convenience sample informs needs and exploratory segment descriptions. Its proportions do not establish Oslo market shares. A named starting cohort retains the model's baseline replacement inflow, organizational costs and logistics capacity.
