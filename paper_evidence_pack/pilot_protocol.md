# Hub and Book pilot protocol

## Decision

Test whether paid pickup assurance can achieve discounted economic value at least 1 times the selected path's maximum cash need over 36 months and discounted payback by month 24, while providing a reliable and relevant customer service. This is a normative capital-allocation policy, chosen independently of the case result; it is not an observed Voi hurdle or an empirically estimated optimum. The current improvement calculation is +NOK 0.95m with june 2028 (month 21) and capital efficiency 1.53. This is an investment appraisal to update, not a measured treatment effect.

## Implementation and timing

Illustrative schedule: first operating month October 2026; setup September 2026. Calendar labels do not change the model timing.

Launch is January 2027; the first treated cohort completes its ramp in April 2027. Review follows June 2027; expansion starts in July 2027 only after a positive decision. Pilot coverage is 30%; 50% of that coverage remains control until release after month 9. Later cohorts each follow their own ramp.

The six-complete-month post-ramp retention window ends in month 13. The current case does not require a retention uplift. Any future retention claim requires a concurrent control through the relevant window, rather than automatic release at the earlier operational gate.

## Assignment and outcomes

Primary outcome: incremental contribution per assigned eligible customer, including those who never book, against a concurrent control. Use stable hub-zone clusters, record supply spillover and geographic contamination, and analyse at the assignment level. Per-booker comparisons do not identify the causal effect of offering the service.

Measure price conversion, bookings per adopter, fulfilment at the promised time with sufficient battery, incremental placement jobs and minutes, marginal transport/support costs, displaced open-market rides, refunds and customer effort/satisfaction. Track competing-app use and reasons for switching separately from satisfaction.

## Gate and financing

Update the full-programme appraisal against both economic targets. Expansion also requires positive future incremental contribution after remaining avoidable costs, feasibility under entered cash/capacity constraints, continued operating rights, and delivery of the service promise. A change to the approved targets requires a board decision. Dedicated placement at 20% is an improvement objective; it is not a substitute for measuring full delivery economics. If any gate fails, redesign or stop: the displayed expansion scenario does not make expansion automatic. Exclude sunk setup from the continuation decision.

Pre-specify sampling, confidence requirements and service guardrails. No sample-size or detection claim is made until baseline, outcome variance, clusters, clustering, allocation and attrition inputs are supplied and checked. Few clusters or interference need specialist design review.
